2. Calculate SEM degrees of freedom

For a full SEM, degrees of freedom are calculated from the number of unique observed covariance elements minus the number of free parameters. This is more appropriate for the structural model shown above than the simplified CFA formula.

Unique observed covariance elements: U = p(p + 1) / 2 For p = 24: U = 24(25) / 2 = 300 Free parameters: 18 + 24 + 4 + 6 + 5 + 2 = 59 Therefore: df_model = 300 − 59 = 241

Why this differs from 237: the earlier simplified formula treated all 6 latent factors as if their variances and all 15 pairwise factor covariances were free. In this structural model, only the 4 exogenous latent variables have freely estimated variances/covariances. TEXP and OTS are endogenous, so their residual/disturbance variances are estimated instead. Consequently, the correct free-parameter count is 59 and the model df is 241.
